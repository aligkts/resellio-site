Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

In short. Resellio turns photos of things you want to sell into ready-to-paste listings. Your photos are used to write the listing and are not stored on our servers. Your listings stay on your phone. We don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

This policy covers the Resellio app for iPhone and Android and this website (together, “Resellio”, “we”, “us”).

What we collect

  • A random account. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account with a random identifier, so it can count your free listings. It has no name or email.
  • Sign-in. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and, if you share it, your email address. Apple lets you hide your real email. On Android, Google sign-in goes through Android’s Credential Manager, and Apple sign-in opens Apple’s sign-in page in a browser tab.
  • Photos you choose to send. When you create a listing, the app sends the photos you picked (made smaller on your phone), the marketplace you chose, the optional note you typed and your app language. See “How listings are written”.
  • Credits and purchases. How many free listings you have used, your pack balance, until when you have Pro, and a record of credits added and spent (with the App Store or Google Play transaction identifier, so a purchase is never counted twice). Apple or Google handles payment; we never see your card.
  • Usage counts. How many listings you created today, to apply fair-use daily limits.

How listings are written

Our server sends your photos and note to an AI model, which writes the title, description, details and a price estimate. The photos and the listing text are held in memory only while the listing is being written; we do not store them on our servers.

Your listings and their photos are saved on your phone only. “Clean background” and “Cut out item” run entirely on your phone; those photos are not sent anywhere unless you use them in a new listing. On Android they use Google’s ML Kit, whose model Google Play services downloads once and then runs on the device.

If a photo shows a person wearing an item, that photo is sent like any other. Only send photos you have the right to use.

Listings are written automatically and can contain mistakes. Check every detail, and the price, before you publish a listing.

Services we use

These companies process data on our behalf, only to run Resellio:

  • Supabase stores your account and credits (servers in Frankfurt, Germany) and runs our server functions.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that writes listings. It receives the photos and note being processed, not your account details. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • RevenueCat handles purchases and subscriptions. It receives your Resellio account identifier and your App Store or Google Play purchase history.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in, and handle payments through the App Store and Google Play.
  • Google Firebase provides analytics and crash reports in the iPhone app (below).

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Where the law requires it, they do so under their data processing terms for international transfers.

Analytics and crash reports

The iPhone app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to see which features are used and to fix crashes. They receive:

  • Feature counts. For example that onboarding was finished, a marketplace was picked, photos were added (and how many), a listing was created (marketplace, number of photos, whether you added a note, a rough time range and a broad category such as “fashion” or “electronics”), a field was copied, the paywall was shown, or a purchase ended (which product and whether it went through). Never the photos, the listing text, the brand, your note, your email or your account identifier.
  • Crash reports, with the last few feature names before the crash.
  • Device details: device model, iOS version, app version, language, a coarse country derived from your IP address, and a random app-instance identifier that is not linked to your account.

You can turn analytics off at any time in the app: Menu → Privacy → “Share usage data”. Crash reports stay on, because they contain no usage data and help us fix problems for everyone. Analytics data is kept for 2 months, the shortest setting Firebase offers. Firebase receives no advertising identifier, advertising features are off, and the data is not used to track you across apps or websites or shared with Google for its own purposes.

The Android app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service. Google Play may show us its own aggregated crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) from users who chose to share them with Google.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and credit records until you delete your account. Deleted data can stay in encrypted backups for a short time before it is overwritten. Daily usage counts are only used for the day they count. Photos and listing text are not kept on our servers at all. To make sure a purchase is never credited twice, we keep a list of store transaction identifiers already credited; it holds no account identifier.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to alicious.apps@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or Türkiye), you have further rights under local law, including the right to object and to complain to your data protection authority; we honour them on request. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the menu and tap “Delete account”. This deletes your account and Resellio’s credit and purchase records from our servers. On iPhone, if you signed in with Apple, it also revokes Resellio’s access to your Apple sign-in; the Android app can’t do this yet, so remove Resellio under “Sign in with Apple” in your Apple Account settings. Unused pack listings are lost. Listings saved on your phone are removed from the app at the same time.

The purchase history that RevenueCat, Apple and Google keep stays with them, as does the list of credited transaction identifiers described above. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: manage it in your iPhone’s Settings or in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions). See also Delete your account.

If you never signed in, your anonymous account holds only your listing counts. Write to us to have it deleted.

Children

Resellio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
