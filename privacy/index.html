Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

In short. Resellio turns photos of things you want to sell into ready-to-paste listings. Your photos are used to write the listing and are not stored on our servers. Your listings stay on your iPhone. We don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

This policy covers the Resellio app for iPhone and this website (together, “Resellio”, “we”, “us”).

What we collect

  • A random account. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account with a random identifier, so it can count your free listings. It has no name or email.
  • Sign-in. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and, if you share it, your email address. Apple lets you hide your real email.
  • Photos you choose to send. When you create a listing, the app sends the photos you picked (made smaller on your iPhone), the marketplace you chose, the optional note you typed and your app language. See “How listings are written”.
  • Credits and purchases. How many free listings you have used, your pack balance, until when you have Pro, and a record of credits added and spent (with the App Store transaction identifier, so a purchase is never counted twice). Apple handles payment; we never see your card.
  • Usage counts. How many listings you created today, to apply fair-use daily limits.

How listings are written

Our server sends your photos and note to an AI model, which writes the title, description, details and a price estimate. The photos and the listing text are held in memory only while the listing is being written; we do not store them on our servers.

Your listings and their photos are saved on your iPhone only. “Clean background” and “Cut out item” run entirely on your iPhone; those photos are not sent anywhere unless you use them in a new listing.

If a photo shows a person wearing an item, that photo is sent like any other. Only send photos you have the right to use.

Listings are written automatically and can contain mistakes. Check every detail, and the price, before you publish a listing.

Services we use

These companies process data on our behalf, only to run Resellio:

  • Supabase stores your account and credits (servers in Frankfurt, Germany) and runs our server functions.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that writes listings. It receives the photos and note being processed, not your account details. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • RevenueCat handles purchases and subscriptions. It receives your Resellio account identifier and your App Store purchase history.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in, and Apple handles payments.
  • Google Firebase provides analytics and crash reports (below).

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Where the law requires it, they do so under their data processing terms for international transfers.

Analytics and crash reports

Resellio uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. They receive which screens you open and which features you use (for example that a listing was created for a given marketplace and how many photos it had, never the photos, the listing text or anything you typed), crash reports, device model, operating system and app version, and a random app-instance identifier. This data is not used for advertising and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and credit records until you delete your account. Deleted data can stay in encrypted backups for a short time before it is overwritten. Daily usage counts are only used for the day they count. Photos and listing text are not kept on our servers at all.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to alicious.apps@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or Türkiye), you have further rights under local law, including the right to object and to complain to your data protection authority; we honour them on request. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the menu and tap “Delete account”. This deletes your account, credits and purchase records from our servers and, if you signed in with Apple, revokes Resellio’s access to your Apple sign-in. Unused pack listings are lost. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage that in your iPhone’s Settings. Listings saved on your iPhone are removed from the app at the same time.

If you never signed in, your anonymous account holds only your listing counts. Write to us to have it deleted.

Children

Resellio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
