Support

Questions, problems or feedback about Resellio.

Write to alicious.apps@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, the marketplace you picked and what happened. Please don’t send passwords or payment details.

How do free listings work?

Every new user gets 3 free listings, no account needed. After that you can sign in and buy a pack (30 or 100 listings) or Resellio Pro. If writing a listing fails, it is not counted.

“Free listings are paused for today”

On very busy days free listings pause until midnight UTC. Your free listings are still yours: try again tomorrow, or use a pack or Pro, which always work.

I bought something but don’t see it

It can take a few seconds to show up. Close and reopen the app. If you have Pro on another account or device, open the menu and tap “Restore purchases” while signed in with the same account.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Resellio. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

A listing has a mistake

Listings are written by AI and can be wrong. Edit any field before you copy it. If a brand, size or material isn’t visible in the photos, Resellio leaves it for you to fill in instead of guessing.

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu and tap “Delete account”. This removes your account and credits from our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.
