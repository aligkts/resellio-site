Delete your account

For the Resellio app on iPhone and Android.

Open the app, tap the menu, then “Delete account”. Or write to alicious.apps@gmail.com from the email address of your account and we will delete it for you.

In the app

  1. Open Resellio and tap the menu button.
  2. Tap “Delete account” (it shows for accounts you signed in to with Apple or Google).
  3. Read the notice and confirm.

The account is deleted on our servers first. If your connection fails, nothing is deleted and you can try again.

By email

If you can’t open the app, write to alicious.apps@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, and say you want your Resellio account deleted. We will confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its sign-in link to Apple or Google.
  • Your remaining free, pack and Pro listing credits.
  • Resellio’s purchase and credit records for the account.
  • The listing history and photos stored on your device are removed by the app when you delete in the app.

What is not deleted

A subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account. Cancel Pro in Google Play (Play Store, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions) on Android, or in your iPhone’s Settings (your name, Subscriptions) on iPhone. Payments already made are handled by Google or Apple, and we can’t refund them by deleting the account. The purchase history that RevenueCat, Google and Apple keep stays with them. We keep a list of store transaction identifiers already credited, without any account identifier, so a purchase is never credited twice, and a daily total of free listings across all users, which does not identify anyone. If you signed in with Apple on Android, also remove Resellio under “Sign in with Apple” in your Apple Account settings; the iPhone app does this for you. See the Privacy Policy.
